BEFORE YOU ASK

A little clarity.
Then, the ocean.

A few helpful starting points for your next board, booking, or visit.

Have another question? Let’s talk ↗
01Can you help me choose a board?+

Absolutely. Tell us your experience level, the waves you usually surf, and what you want from your next board. We can help you narrow down the shapes.

02What should I include about my order?+

Include your order number, the email used at checkout, and a short description of your question. Please leave out payment card details.

03Where do I book a rental or lesson?+

Explore our rentals or lessons pages to get started. Contact us if you need help choosing an option.

04Can I see the boards in person?+

Visit the shop during opening hours to explore the collection. Call ahead if there’s a particular model or size you’d like to see.

05Who can help with delivery or returns?+

Email hello@wavecraft.com with your order details and question. Our team can explain the options that apply to your purchase.
