EQUIPMENT / EXPLORE

Surfboard Rentals
The details that matter.

A rental starts with a conversation about you, not just a board on a rack. Share what you normally ride, your confidence in the water, and how long you plan to surf. The team can discuss suitable equipment and confirm what is available for your dates.

Who it suits

Independent surfers looking for equipment for a coastal visit, or riders exploring a different board shape.

Why explore it

Spend less time transporting equipment and more time choosing a setup suited to your session.

OPTIONS & PRACTICAL DETAILS

Make it work for you.

01

Choose a shape

Discuss soft tops, longer boards, or more responsive shapes based on experience. Specific models depend on availability.

02

Find your fit

Share your height, approximate weight, paddling confidence, and usual board. These details help guide the equipment conversation.

03

Plan the rental

Confirm collection, return timing, accessories, care requirements, and charges before committing.

These are topics to discuss, not confirmed inclusions. Contact us for availability and pricing.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you make plans.

Can you help me choose a board?+

Yes—start by sharing your experience, usual board, and the type of waves you plan to surf. Confirm the final choice and availability with the team.

Is a wetsuit included?+

Do not assume it is. Ask which accessories are available and whether they carry a separate charge.

Can I keep the board for several days?+

Ask about your preferred dates and return time. The team will confirm available rental periods and pricing.
