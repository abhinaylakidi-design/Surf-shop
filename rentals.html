Ask us anything ↗
Which board should I choose?

For first waves, explore First Light. For easy cruising, try Coastal Cruiser or Tide Explorer. Confident surfers can start with Ocean Runner. Our crew can help refine the fit.

Can I rent if I’m new to surfing?

Start with a stable soft top and talk to our crew about your experience. A lesson is a great way to build confidence before your first independent session. Explore lessons ↗

What should I bring for pickup?

Bring your booking details and your beach essentials. Confirm any identification, deposit, and equipment requirements with the team before you arrive.

Can I keep my board for longer?

Ask the crew before your agreed return time. Extensions depend on board availability and the applicable rental rate.
