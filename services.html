BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

A little clarity.
A better start.

Good questions make for thoughtful plans. Here are a few things to know before your next session.

Ask us something else ↗
Which service is suitable for a beginner?+

Beginner Surf Lessons are the best place to start the conversation. Share your water confidence and ask the team to confirm a suitable format and equipment.

How do I choose a rental board?+

Tell us what you normally ride, your experience, and your plans. Ask which shapes and sizes are available before deciding on your setup.

What should I bring?+

Ask about equipment inclusions and bring the personal items agreed with the team. Swimwear, a towel, water, and suitable sun protection are useful starting points.

Can I arrange a private or group session?+

Tell the team your preferred dates, goals, and participant numbers. Private and group arrangements depend on the available format and staffing.

How do ocean conditions affect a session?+

Conditions can affect timing, location, or whether a session can go ahead. Confirm how updates, changes, and cancellations are handled before booking.

How do I ask about repairs?+

Send clear photos and a description of your board and damage. Repair services are proposed; confirm assessment availability and capability with the team.
