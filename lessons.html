04 / BEFORE YOU PADDLE OUT

A few questions.
A little clarity.

Getting ready for your first session? Start here, or talk to us about what you need.

Ask the Team ↗
Do I need any surfing experience?+

No surfing experience is needed for beginner lessons. Let the team know your swimming ability and comfort in the ocean before booking.

Which lesson should I choose?+

Start with beginner lessons if you’re new to surfing. Choose intermediate to develop turns and wave selection, or private coaching for a more individual focus.

What should I bring?+

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, and drinking water. Confirm equipment, wetsuit needs, and the meeting point with the team before your session.

What if the conditions change?+

Surf sessions depend on ocean and weather conditions. Contact the team to confirm your session and discuss the options if conditions aren’t suitable.

Can you help me choose my own board?+

Yes. Tell us about your progress and the waves you want to ride, and explore the shop for a board that suits your next step.
